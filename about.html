Artists Mission

Hi! I'm Sabrina Guzman, an artist based in New York City. My work explores themes of identity, culture, and the human experience through vibrant colors and dynamic compositions. I draw inspiration from my Puerto Rican heritage and the diverse urban environment around me. Through my art, I aim to create a dialogue that resonates with viewers on a personal level.
