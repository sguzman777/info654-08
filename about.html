Artists Mission

Hi! I’m an artist based in New York City. My work explores themes of femininity and the symbolism of eyes, brought to life through vibrant colors and dynamic compositions. I draw inspiration from the diverse urban environment I grew up in and carry forward my mission of spreading freedom of expression through both my art and my teaching. Through my work, I aim to create bold, unique pieces that showcase my skills in line, color theory, and design, while inspiring others to embrace their own creativity.
