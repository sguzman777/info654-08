Artworks created by: Sabrina Guzman

My art portfolio is a celebration of color, imagination, and individuality. Each piece bursts with vivid hues, bold compositions, and intricate designs that draw the viewer into worlds that feel both familiar and otherworldly. I’m fascinated by eyes—to me, they are the portals that reveal our deepest truths and emotions even when words fall short. My work often plays with this idea, inviting viewers to look closer, not only at my art but also within themselves. I love creating pieces that make people pause and wonder, “How was that made?”—blurring the line between the real and the surreal. My art is an expression of my weirdness, curiosity, and pride in seeing life through a unique lens; it’s about embracing what makes me different and turning that into something beautiful, thought-provoking, and unapologetically me.
